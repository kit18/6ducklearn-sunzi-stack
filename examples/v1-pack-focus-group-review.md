The pack's own quality gate (`sunzi-focus-group-skill-review`) run against the
v1.0.0 architecture. Scores are deterministic, produced by
`scripts/score-user-intention.mjs`, not asserted by the model.

## Panel Design

- Review label: **synthetic panel simulation, not real quotes**
- Review goal: is the v1.0.0 campaign-loop architecture adoptable, or is it
  well-built and unusable?
- Panel roles: solo founder (pre-revenue), solo founder (experienced,
  agent-native), independent consultant, hostile skeptic, corporate strategy
  lead, agent-platform integrator, ops lead who lives with the outputs,
  non-Chinese-reading enterprise user

## Cross-Panel Synthesis

- **Consensus**: evidence discipline and output actionability are strong across
  every archetype including the hostile one. The pack's differentiators —
  computed economics, first-class `do not start`, the restraint gate — land.
- **Dissent**: the consultant scores 8.9 and the corporate lead 6.1 on the same
  artifact. The disagreement is real and unresolved by argument: a consultant
  bills for depth; an internal team is punished for it. Evidence that would
  resolve it: whether an internal team actually completes a loop unassisted.
- **Blind spot**: the pack optimizes for the decision that deserves six stages
  and has no sanctioned shape for the decision that deserves one.
- **Adoption risk**: ceremony-avoidance — teams routing around the pack rather
  than running it partially.
- **Safety / trust risk**: none surfaced; the legitimacy line and
  anti-weaponization rules were read as strengths.

## Decision

**Verdict: revise**
Confidence: medium (synthetic panel; no real usage evidence)

## Recommended Changes

1. **ASCII artifact filenames** — artifacts must be named
   `miaosuan-scorecard.md` and `miaosuan-recalibration.md`, not with CJK
   characters in the filename. Classical terms belong inside the file where
   they teach, not in a path a user has to type. *(highest priority: it
   silently excludes a whole market)* — **applied in v1.0.1**
2. **A sanctioned quick path** — name the single-stage entry points explicitly
   so partial use is legitimate rather than a workaround. — **applied in v1.0.1**
3. **Trigger routing** — the orchestrator stated as the default for anyone who
   cannot map their question, in the skills' own text and not just the README.
   — **applied in v1.0.1**

The retest below has not been run; these are the changes the panel asked for,
and the panel should be re-run against them rather than assumed fixed.

**What did not move, and should not be papered over:** the skeptic's
`framing_accessibility` stayed at 2. Classical chapter headings still read as
decoration to a reader who dislikes the genre, and no filename change fixes
that. The honest position is that the framing earns its place through the
structure it supplies (五事 as an audit taxonomy, 九地 as a position
classifier, 火攻 as an irreversibility gate) and not through the headings
themselves — a reader who wants the structure without the vocabulary is served
by the English skill names and can ignore the rest.

The corporate lead at 6.9 remains the lowest score and the honest open
question: whether an internal team completes a loop unassisted is still
unmeasured.

**Method limitation.** This is a synthetic retest: the panel is simulated and
the revised signals were assigned by mapping each shipped fix to the objection
it addressed. It demonstrates that the fixes target the raised objections. It
is not evidence that real users respond this way, and the aggregate movement
(+0.5) should be read as a design check, not a result.
