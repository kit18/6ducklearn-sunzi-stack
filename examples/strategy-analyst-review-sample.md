# Strategy Analyst Review Sample

## Strategy Under Review

The proposal is to enter a partner marketplace by giving the partner an exclusive integration for six months, then use the integration to become the system of record for the partner's merchants.

## Verdict

Verdict: revise

Confidence: medium

## Evidence Audit

| Claim | Evidence | Quality | Missing / falsifier |
|---|---|---|---|
| Partner marketplace has qualified demand | Pipeline anecdotes and two merchant interviews | Medium | Usage data, attach rate, churn, support burden |
| Exclusivity will unlock distribution | Partner requested a differentiated offer | Medium | Written promotion commitment and placement terms |
| System-of-record expansion is feasible | Product has partial workflow coverage | Weak | Migration cost, data rights, customer consent, support load |

## Lens Review

| Lens | Fit | Review |
|---|---|---|
| 假道伐虢 | Marketplace access can become leverage | Valid only if consent, platform rules, and customer value are explicit |
| 反客為主 | Edge entrant can earn control by owning workflow | Must be earned through value, not covert capture |
| 連環計 | Sequenced integration, distribution, and workflow expansion may compound | Risk of correlated dependency on one partner |

## Competing Diagnosis

The real bottleneck may not be marketplace access. It may be incomplete workflow coverage and high migration friction. If true, exclusivity increases dependency before the product can retain merchants.

## Ethical Rewrite

Replace "use access to become system of record" with "earn expansion through explicit merchant opt-in, data portability, transparent terms, and measurable workflow value."

## Required Revisions

1. Define partner promotion commitments before granting exclusivity.
2. Add customer consent and data-rights language.
3. Set a non-exclusive fallback if attach rate is weak after 60 days.
4. Track activation, retained workflow usage, support cost, and merchant opt-in rate.
5. Kill the exclusive path if partner promotion does not ship by the agreed date.
