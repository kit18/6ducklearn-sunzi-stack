# Industry Leader Smoke Test: Sunzi Consultation Cases

This artifact is a synthetic industry-leader agent smoke test, not real quotes. It runs the Sunzi consultation stack end to end across four public-safe industry archetypes: B2B SaaS / PLG growth, ecommerce / retail operations, fintech / regulated financial services, and enterprise AI platform / developer tooling.

## Smoke Test Contract

- Skills run end to end: `sunzi-strategy-consultant`, `sunzi-stakeholder-ssr`, `sunzi-focus-group-skill-review`, and `strategy-analyst-review`.
- Review label: synthetic industry-leader agent smoke test, not real quotes.
- Objective: ask each industry archetype for Top 3 Problems, select one smoke-testable case, run SSR, run focus-group review, run strategy analyst gate, and produce a successful case only after revisions.
- Pass condition: each selected case includes a strategy diagnosis, Definition Of Done, Synthetic SSR Output, Focus Group Review, Strategy Analyst Gate, Evidence Needed Next, metrics, kill criteria, and final state `works after revision`.
- Blocked moves: fake proof, real-person impersonation, hidden terms, coercive growth, regulatory shortcut, private-data use, and synthetic output presented as customer evidence.

## Research Metrics Safety Check

- Use `synthetic_proxy` for industry-leader archetype comments, focus-group reactions, and 360 comments.
- Generate Interview Responses and 360 Comments before drafting any dynamic scoring contract.
- Freeze the formula at `after_comments_before_structured_signal_extraction` before structured signal extraction.
- Calculate Deterministic Scores with the Node.js scorer when a case needs buying intention, NPS proxy, PMF proxy, willingness-to-pay proxy, adoption intent, switching cost, task-fit, or custom criteria.
- Do not present synthetic scores as real NPS, real PMF, real willingness-to-pay proof, market proof, customer evidence, or percent-would-buy evidence.
- `works after revision` means ready for real validation, not validated by synthetic intent.


## Case 1: AI Feature Commoditization Repositioning

Industry: B2B SaaS / PLG growth.

### Sunzi Strategy Consultant

- Decision: reposition a generic AI analytics assistant so it competes on a defensible workflow instead of crowded AI claims.
- Terrain: buyers see many similar assistant claims, trial users do not understand the first useful action, and sales absorbs differentiation objections.
- Actors: founder, PMM lead, growth lead, sales lead, buyer champion, skeptical economic buyer.
- Strategic lens: avoid the crowded battlefield and strike the ignored workflow.
- Modern legal move: replace "AI analytics assistant" with "decision-ready revenue evidence for weekly pipeline calls"; ship one proof artifact and one activation CTA.

### Definition Of Done

- Names the ignored buyer workflow and the current switching objection.
- Includes PMM, growth, sales, buyer, support, and trust/safety reactions.
- Blocks exaggerated AI capability claims and fake customer proof.
- Defines evidence needed before scaling: visitor-to-start, start-to-activation, activation-to-team-invite, and objection-quality change.
- Includes retreat trigger if activation does not improve without increasing misleading claims.

### Synthetic SSR Output

- Scenario: AI assistant differentiation is weak and paid conversion is flat.
- Stakeholders: PMM lead, trial user, sales lead, buyer champion, trust/safety reviewer.
- Trigger: repositioning from generic AI dashboard to revenue-evidence workflow.
- Label: synthetic stakeholder simulation, not a real quote.
- Evidence boundary: hypothesis, not proof.

| Stakeholder | Synthetic reaction | Risk exposed | Safe response |
|---|---|---|---|
| PMM lead | "The new wedge is clearer, but we need proof that pipeline-call evidence is the moment buyers remember." | Messaging may sound precise but remain unproven | Run a copy test and demo-call objection review |
| Trial user | "I know what to try now: prepare one pipeline call. I still need sample data and a realistic first action." | Activation fails if setup is heavy | Provide sample workflow and one narrow first action |

Verdict: go after revision.

| Claim | Evidence | Quality | Missing / falsifier |

- Leading metric: first useful artifact completion.
- Lagging metric: activated account to team invite.

Success evidence is activation lift, repeat weekly artifact creation, better sales objection quality, and no increase in misleading AI claims.

## Cross-Industry Synthesis

- Repeated top problem: teams mistake visible tactics for terrain. AI claims, sitewide discounts, transaction holds, and agent demos all look like action while hiding the real bottleneck.
- Repeated Sunzi move: shift the battlefield from noisy head-on competition to the constraint that determines success: buyer workflow, margin architecture, fraud network pressure, or governed access point.
- Strongest dissent: support and trust/safety roles repeatedly warned that strategy failure is paid for by frontline teams and user trust.
- End-to-end result: each case moved from broad industry pressure to tested operating plan after SSR, focus-group dissent, and strategy analyst gate.
- Final status: works after revision.
