# Growth Community Stakeholder SSR

## Scenario

A workflow app has rising community traffic after an AI tutorial post, but activation is flat. The growth team wants to automate daily community posts and add an urgent discount banner. The simulated stakeholders are a community moderator, a power user, and a support lead who will absorb complaints if the tactic feels spammy or manipulative.

## Artifact 1: Community Moderator Reaction

Label: synthetic artifact, not a real message.

```text
[#moderation-backchannel]

Moderator:
I saw the proposed daily posting schedule from `[Workflow App]`. One thoughtful tutorial was useful. Daily automated posts are different.

The issue is not that vendors cannot share tools here. The issue is community trust. If every product team turns one good post into a recurring promotion, members stop reading the channel.

Also, the urgent discount banner makes this feel less like education and more like pressure. If activation is flat, posting more often will not solve the value problem. It will just make the community carry the cost.

Safe response: one clearly labeled educational post, one reusable template, no urgency framing, and a promise to stop if replies or spam reports turn negative.
```

## Strategic Read

- Hidden terrain: the channel is working as attention, but not yet as activation. Community goodwill is the scarce asset.
- Root objection: "Do not make us pay for your weak onboarding loop."
- Likely escalation path: moderator warning -> public user pushback -> support complaints -> growth rollback request.
- Technical blind spot: the team may optimize posting cadence without measuring first meaningful action completion by source cohort.
- Trust repair move: ask moderator permission, publish one useful artifact, state commercial intent plainly, and stop if trust guardrails move.
- Safe response: replace automation with a reversible value-led experiment; track activation, day-7 retained workflow use, spam reports, negative replies, and support burden.
- Test signal: safe response is present only if the tactic preserves community trust while proving activation.

## Evidence Needed Next

- Moderator review before posting.
- Cohort activation from the original tutorial traffic.
- Five observed sessions from community users attempting the first workflow.
- Support-tag baseline for promo-related complaints.
