# Full Campaign Loop: DTC Consumable Launch

A complete run of 審 → 算 → 謀 → 驗 → 斷 → 省 on one decision, showing every
stage artifact and how they chain.

**Synthetic case.** The company, numbers, and rival are constructed to
exercise the loop's mechanics — in particular the unit-economics gate
catching a cost target that looks like a floor and is actually a trap. It is
not a real client engagement.

Decision gated: *should we launch the subscription starter kit in Q4?*

---

## 審 — Foundation Audit

Orchestrator triage: 道 and 法 are load-bearing (the thesis is a repeat-purchase
mechanism and the economics are unproven). 天 and 地 run as quick checks. 將 is
skipped — the operator has run two prior launches in this category and the
decision is not command-limited.

### mandate-audit.md (道) — deep

| Constituency | Asked to risk | Structurally given | Followership evidence (tier) | Dissent channel |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Target customers | $39 + a recurring commitment | A routine that saves ~10 min/week | 41 priced preorders from 900 qualified visitors (probe) | — |
| Ops lead | Q4 capacity during peak | No relief on existing SLAs | Verbal agreement only (management impression) | Named: ops lead, weekly 1:1 |

Pain reality: claimed pain is "the weekly routine is annoying." Behavioral
evidence is the 41 priced preorders — real money, but a single probe.
**What the evidence cannot yet show: whether anyone buys the second box.**

Verdict: **conditional**. The condition is a repeat-purchase proxy.

### window-assessment.md (天) — quick check

Claimed window: "Q4 gifting season." Closing event: Dec 24 — real and dated.
**Runway test**: claimed close date Dec 24; cash-out date March. These do not
suspiciously agree. Urgency reads **market-real**, not runway.

Verdict: **strike**, with the caveat that Q4 is the hardest CAC quarter of the
year — the window that opens for demand also opens for everyone's ad spend.

### terrain-survey.md (地) — quick check

Ground classification: **爭地** — contested category, three funded entrants,
advantageous to whoever holds it. Doctrine: 爭地則無攻 — do not attack a held
position head-on; arrive first or not at all.

Supply lines: one paid channel (Meta) carries 80% of planned volume; one
contract manufacturer carries 100% of the kit. Both cuttable. Early signal on
the channel: CPM drift above $32.

### operating-model.md (法) — deep, PROVISIONING mode

Daily fully-loaded campaign cost: **$4,100/day** (±15%) at planned spend.

**Unit Economics Gate**

- Price / AOV:            $39.00
- Landed COGS:            $17.55  (source: manufacturer quote at 500u)
- Payment + fulfillment:  $10.93  (2.9%+$0.30; $7.50 ship; $2.00 pick/pack)
- Returns reserve:        $1.17   (rate assumed: 3%)
- Contribution:           $9.35
- Break-even CAC:         $9.35
- Allowable CAC:          $7.95   (target margin: 15%)
- Category CAC range:     $25–$45 (source: three published category benchmarks + our own Q2 test at $31)
- Payback period:         never on first order; 2.1 orders at current contribution
- Verdict on economics:   **not viable**
- Kill criterion:         blended CAC above $13 for 7 consecutive days

**The finding.** The team carried $17.55 as "maximum acceptable COGS." At that
COGS the offer loses money on every acquired order at any realistic category
CAC — the number is not a floor, it is a trap. 因糧於敵 check surfaced two
options: negotiate COGS to ≤$11.70 at 2,000u, or raise AOV to $59–69 by
launching the bundle rather than the single kit.

Incentive conflict: the growth lead's Q4 bonus is indexed to revenue, not
contribution — a metric that actively fights this plan.

## 謀 — The Maneuver

### maneuver-brief.md — 謀攻 ladder

| Rung | Concrete move available | Price | Sufficient? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 伐謀 | Make the incumbent's advantage irrelevant: sell the *bundle* as a gift SKU where their subscription depth doesn't compete | ~$0; repackaging | **Yes, partially** — raises AOV to $64, fixing the economics without a fight |
| 伐交 | Retail partner for Q4 shelf; co-marketing with a complementary brand | 4–6 weeks, margin share | Viable but too slow for this window |
| 伐兵 | Direct paid contest with Northwind on Meta in Q4 | $25–45 CAC vs. $7.95 allowable | **No** — the count says we lose this |
| 攻城 | Sustained price war for subscribers | Category price level | No 不得已 justification exists |

Force ratio (rows 5–6): inferior on capital and cost base. Sizing doctrine:
少則能逃之 — with inferior force, evasion is skill. **Chosen rung: 伐謀.**

**Selected lens: `暗渡陳倉`** — the visible move (a Q4 gift SKU) covers the real
advance (acquiring subscription customers at gift-buyer economics, where the
incumbent's retention advantage does not price). Ethical translation: the gift
SKU is a genuine product honestly sold; the "cover" is competitive positioning,
not customer deception. Falsifier: if gift buyers never convert to refills, this
is a one-time revenue spike wearing a subscription thesis.

### 虛實 wedge

| Their strength | Commits them to | The 虛 it creates |
|---|---|---|
| 60k subscriber base, 14-mo tenure | Subscription-first messaging and pricing | Gifting is off-strategy for them; a gift buyer is a bad subscriber on their metrics |

Closing-cost test: to contest gifting, Northwind must build non-subscription
SKUs and accept lower LTV cohorts — which dilutes the retention metric their
whole story rests on. **Expensive for them. Wedge kept.**

正 (must not lose): fulfillment reliability through Q4 peak — funded, owned by ops lead.
奇 (wins): the gift-bundle framing at $64 AOV.

### 九變 contingency

| Loss scenario | Earliest signal | Watcher | Pre-agreed adaptation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gift buyers don't convert to refills | First-refill rate < 18% at day 45 | Growth lead | Stop refill spend; reprice as one-time gift business |
| CPM inflation eats the margin gain | Blended CAC > $13 for 7 days | Growth lead | Pause cold; shift to retargeting + owned |
| Manufacturer misses Q4 lead time | PO confirmation slips past Sep 30 | Ops lead | Cancel Q4 launch, hold for Q1 |

**Refusals (所不):** no discounting below $54 (protects the AOV that makes the
economics work); no scarcity claims about stock we hold; no answering
Northwind publicly if they name us.

Kill layer: blended CAC > $13 for 7 consecutive days. Owner: founder.
**Agrees with the economics gate's kill criterion.**

## 驗 — The Stress Test

**SSR (synthetic — hypothesis, not proof):** the ops lead's simulated reaction
surfaced that Q4 peak plus a new SKU means the 正 (fulfillment reliability) is
under-resourced at current headcount. Evidence needed next: a real capacity
conversation before the PO.

**strategy-analyst-review:** flagged that the first-refill threshold (18%) was
asserted, not derived. Revised to 18% as the break-even refill rate computed
from the $64 AOV contribution — now a derived number with the arithmetic shown.

**DoD calibration:** closest case `Market Entry Without Head-On War`. Source
anchor copied. Definition Of Done gap: our plan had no owner for the retail
conversation that the case's DoD treats as mandatory. Evidence Needed Next:
named owner, or the 伐交 rung stays closed honestly.

## 斷 — The Decision

No irreversible element: the gift SKU is reversible, spend is capped, no
litigation, no layoffs, no price-level damage below the $54 refusal.
**`/sunzi-restraint` not required** — recorded as a deliberate finding rather
than a skipped step.

The decision the loop actually changed: the team arrived wanting to launch a
$39 kit into a Q4 paid contest. The count said they lose that fight, and the
economics gate showed why — the $17.55 COGS ceiling they were treating as a
safety margin guaranteed a loss on every acquired order. The launch proceeds,
at a different price, on different ground, against a weakness the incumbent
cannot close cheaply.

**Lens audit:** `暗渡陳倉` — **earned**. Removing it would have left the team
selling the same kit at the same price to the same buyer.

**Contingency audit:** refusals held. The CAC trigger fired once on Nov 29 and
the pre-agreed pause executed same-day — the layer worked because the watcher
was named.

**Timing audit:** the window assessment predicted Q4 CAC inflation and was
correct; it was not overridden.

**Inheritance (max 3):**
1. We treat first supplier quotes as constraints rather than opening positions —
   run the 因糧於敵 check *before* setting any COGS ceiling.
2. We under-forecast repeat intent from priced-preorder probes by roughly 5 pts;
   adjust probe-tier confidence upward slightly for this segment.
3. Incentive conflicts get named in 法 and then not fixed. Next campaign: no
   `proceed` verdict while a named incentive conflict is unresolved.
