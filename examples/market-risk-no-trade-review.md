# Market-Risk No-Trade Review

## Scenario

A team wants to trade a thinly liquid crypto token after seeing coordinated social posts, sudden volume, and unclear exchange depth.

## Verdict

Verdict: stop

Confidence: high for no-trade until liquidity and manipulation risk are clarified.

## Evidence Audit

| Claim | Evidence | Quality | Missing / falsifier |
|---|---|---|---|
| Momentum is strong | Social posts and volume spike | Weak to medium | Venue-level order book, wash-trade checks |
| Liquidity is sufficient | Claimed by promoters | Weak | Slippage estimate, exit capacity, counterparty concentration |
| Catalyst is real | No primary-source confirmation | Weak | Official filing, protocol update, exchange notice |

## Lens Check

| Lens | Fit | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| 混水摸魚 | Disorder may create mispricing | High misuse risk if the plan depends on confusion or promotion |
| 打草驚蛇 | Probing can expose hidden risk | Use only for small analytical checks, not market-moving behavior |
| 走為上計 | Exit/no-trade preserves optionality | Strong fit because continuation has poor signal integrity |

## Ethical Rewrite

Do not participate in pumping, coordinated promotion, spoofing, wash trading, or misleading claims. Reframe the work as detection and defense:

1. Map liquidity, venue concentration, wallet concentration, and slippage.
2. Require primary-source catalyst confirmation.
3. Set a no-trade rule until manipulation indicators clear.
4. Document invalidation conditions before any future exposure.

## Kill Criteria

- No primary-source catalyst.
- Top wallets or venues dominate flow without transparent explanation.
- Expected slippage exceeds risk budget.
- Social promotion is the main reason for entry.

This is analytical risk review only, not financial advice.
