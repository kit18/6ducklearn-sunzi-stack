# Domain Adapter: Growth And Ecommerce

Pulled in by the 五事 skills, `/sunzi-compare`, and the maneuver tier whenever
the decision is a growth loop, GTM experiment, ecommerce conversion play,
community distribution motion, lifecycle campaign, referral, or acquisition
plan. This adapter supplies the evidence lanes and output checks; the invoking
skill supplies the diagnosis. (Replaces the v0.2.0 `sunzi-growth-review`
skill — see `docs/MIGRATION.md`.)

Core rule: growth is terrain plus feedback. Find the constraint, choose a
reversible move, measure learning, and protect trust.

## Evidence lanes (operating)

- Customer segment, funnel data, channel mix, conversion, retention, margin,
  inventory, service reliability, offer/pricing, competitor pressure.
- Separate demand creation, demand capture, and operational fulfillment —
  three different failure modes that one "growth" number hides.

## 未戰 lane (mandatory when pre-revenue / pre-funnel)

- Probe-tier evidence only, labeled: mock bundles, priced preorders,
  waitlists with skin in the game, supplier sample rounds.
- Proxy metrics must falsify the actual thesis: a retention/refill thesis
  needs a priced retention proxy (subscribe option, refill preorder) — an
  add-to-cart or traffic proxy cannot stand in for it; reject the
  substitution.
- The [unit economics gate](../unit-economics-gate.md) runs on quotes and
  benchmarks with every estimated row marked.

## Required output checks (the invoking skill's artifact must contain)

- Binding growth constraint, stated.
- Mechanism before tactic: why the loop should work, before naming posts,
  ads, discounts, partnerships, or automation.
- Completed unit-economics block for any money campaign: contribution
  ladder, break-even CAC vs. observed category CAC, payback vs. runway.
- One reversible experiment with owner, cohort, leading metric, trust
  guardrail, and one explicit stop rule.
- Evidence ledger with tiers and missing-data rows.

## DoD calibration

Run DoD calibration against the closest real-life case in the
[Real-life DoD case map](../real-life-dod-case-map.md), especially
`Market Entry Without Head-On War`, `Community Growth Without Spam`, or
`Pricing And Packaging Change`: copy the source anchor, compare against the
Definition Of Done, state the Evidence Needed Next.

## Lens discipline

Privately screen the canonical matrix against the growth constraint, timing,
positioning, trust risk, and retreat / stop-rule need, weighted by the
position classification from the 廟算 scorecard. Do not show the screening,
rejected lenses, scores, or full matrix. Output only 1 to 3 lenses whose
mechanism materially improves the experiment design; if none, say
`None selected`. Useful patterns on this ground: `擒賊擒王` (segment or
journey concentration), `圍魏救趙` (acquisition pain that is really
activation or trust pain), `順手牽羊` (low-friction gains inside existing
traffic), `偷樑換柱` (pricing metric, default, checkout, or channel
architecture changes).

## Hard rules

- No fake scarcity, fake reviews, hidden fees, deceptive discounts, spam,
  astroturfing, dark patterns, unsupported claims, or manipulative urgency.
- No channel scaled without a baseline and a stop rule.
- Traffic is not success unless activation, retention, revenue, or learning
  improves.
- Never output a checklist of all 36 stratagems, rejected lenses, or an
  internal screening table.
- A manipulation request reframes toward truthful growth, detection,
  defense, compliance, or customer education.
