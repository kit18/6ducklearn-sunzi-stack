# Thirty-Six Stratagems Matrix

Use this as a lens library. Pick 2 to 4 lenses. Do not dump the whole matrix.

## 勝戰計: Advantage and momentum

| # | Lens | Modern principle | Diagnostic question | Falsifier |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 瞞天過海 | Hide meaningful change inside familiar routines so the other side misprices it as normal. | Is the real move happening inside a process everyone treats as routine? | The visible activity fully explains the value creation or risk. |
| 2 | 圍魏救趙 | Relieve pressure indirectly by attacking the binding constraint. | What pressure can be solved by changing a different part of the system? | The indirect target is not actually connected to the pressure. |
| 3 | 借刀殺人 | Use another actor's incentive or force while preserving your own resources. | Which third party already has reason to move in your desired direction? | The third party has no real incentive or creates unacceptable ethical/legal risk. |
| 4 | 以逸待勞 | Win by controlling pace and position while others exhaust themselves. | Can patience and prepared position beat chasing? | Waiting loses the window or the opponent is not spending energy. |
| 5 | 趁火打劫 | In disruption, move quickly toward exposed bottlenecks or assets. | What valuable thing became reachable because the environment is unstable? | The disruption only raises risk and exposes no durable value. |
| 6 | 聲東擊西 | Shape attention so defenses over-index on the wrong surface. | Where is attention concentrated, and where is the real leverage? | The attention shift does not change behavior or allocation. |

## 敵戰計: Rivalry and contested signals

| # | Lens | Modern principle | Diagnostic question | Falsifier |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 7 | 無中生有 | Use a probe or small signal to discover demand, then convert real response into leverage. | Can a low-cost test create real information before commitment? | Response is vanity interest with no behavior, money, or workflow change. |
| 8 | 暗渡陳倉 | The visible move is cover; the real advance happens through a quieter path. | Is the public action masking a more important operating path? | The quiet path has no resource flow, capability build, or strategic value. |
| 9 | 隔岸觀火 | Let rival disorder reveal the better move before intervening. | What will we learn by waiting for internal conflict to surface? | Waiting lets the problem spread or the opportunity disappear. |
| 10 | 笑裏藏刀 | Friendly posture can hide adversarial intent; judge incentives and preparation. | Do incentives contradict the friendly message? | There is no hidden capability, preparation, or incentive conflict. |
| 11 | 李代桃僵 | Sacrifice a lower-value piece to protect the core position. | What small loss prevents a catastrophic loss? | The sacrifice damages the core or avoids no meaningful risk. |
| 12 | 順手牽羊 | Capture small certain gains exposed by larger movement. | What low-friction value is exposed while doing the main work? | The small gain distracts from the core objective. |

## 攻戰計: Discovery and pressure

| # | Lens | Modern principle | Diagnostic question | Falsifier |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 13 | 打草驚蛇 | Probe cheaply to reveal hidden risk or intent. | What small test forces the hidden actor or constraint to show itself? | The probe alerts a rival without revealing useful information. |
| 14 | 借屍還魂 | Revive abandoned or underused assets as new leverage. | What existing asset could become valuable in a new context? | The asset is obsolete for structural reasons, not just neglected. |
| 15 | 調虎離山 | Do not fight strength where it is strongest; change the battlefield. | How can we move the problem away from the opponent's advantage? | The new battlefield favors the opponent just as much. |
| 16 | 欲擒故縱 | Avoid over-pressure; let the target exhaust itself before commitment. | Where does patience make the other side reveal need or weakness? | Delay reduces trust or lets the target escape permanently. |
| 17 | 拋磚引玉 | Offer a small thing to draw a higher-value response. | What cheap offer can reveal demand, information, or commitment? | The response does not create new information or leverage. |
| 18 | 擒賊擒王 | Target the controlling node, not every symptom. | What actor, process, or constraint controls the whole system? | The supposed control node is only a visible symptom. |

## 混戰計: Disorder and multi-party conflict

| # | Lens | Modern principle | Diagnostic question | Falsifier |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 19 | 釜底抽薪 | Remove the fuel sustaining visible force. | What keeps the current problem alive? | Removing the fuel is slower or costlier than direct resolution. |
| 20 | 混水摸魚 | Disorder creates mispriced assets, forced behavior, and decision errors. | Who is mispricing value because the environment is chaotic? | Disorder also destroys the ability to execute safely. |
| 21 | 金蟬脫殼 | Preserve confidence while moving the real asset or effort elsewhere. | What must remain stable while the core moves? | The shell misleads stakeholders or creates trust damage. |
| 22 | 關門捉賊 | Contain the threat before optimizing the attack. | What escape, spread, or contagion path must be closed first? | Containment traps you with a bigger risk than the threat itself. |
| 23 | 遠交近攻 | Partner with distant actors while focusing effort on nearby threats. | Which distant ally buys time or leverage against the near threat? | The distant ally has no relevance to the immediate constraint. |
| 24 | 假道伐虢 | Use access granted for one purpose to reach the actual strategic position. | What access path changes the control point? | Access violates consent, contracts, data boundaries, or trust. |

## 並戰計: Alliances, substitutions, and control shifts

| # | Lens | Modern principle | Diagnostic question | Falsifier |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25 | 偷樑換柱 | Replace the load-bearing component so the system changes beneath the surface. | Which default, metric, vendor, or process quietly controls outcomes? | The component is not load-bearing. |
| 26 | 指桑罵槐 | Send a visible message to one target to discipline another. | Who is the real audience for this visible action? | The real audience cannot observe or respond to the signal. |
| 27 | 假癡不癲 | Underplay knowledge and avoid premature action until timing improves. | Where does restraint preserve optionality? | Silence causes loss of trust, legal risk, or missed opportunity. |
| 28 | 上屋抽梯 | Create commitment, then remove easy retreat. | What commitment mechanism makes success more likely? | The commitment is coercive, dishonest, or harms retention. |
| 29 | 樹上開花 | Borrow structure or credibility to amplify a small force. | What external structure makes a small move credible? | The borrowed credibility is cosmetic and unsupported by substance. |
| 30 | 反客為主 | Enter at the edge, then earn the operating center. | How can a peripheral position become the control layer through value? | Control is captured without consent or without creating value. |

## 敗戰計: Downside, exit, and recovery

| # | Lens | Modern principle | Diagnostic question | Falsifier |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 31 | 美人計 | Strong actors can be weakened by distraction, vanity, or poor governance. | What desire or vanity metric is degrading decision quality? | The supposed distraction is not changing allocation or behavior. |
| 32 | 空城計 | When weak, ambiguity and reputation may buy time. | Can credible ambiguity preserve options without lying? | Ambiguity becomes deception or delays necessary action. |
| 33 | 反間計 | Existing mistrust can cause the other side's information system to fail. | Where is the opponent already divided or distrustful? | There is no real mistrust or using it would cross ethical/legal lines. |
| 34 | 苦肉計 | Costly signals can build credibility but can become self-harm. | What sacrifice proves commitment without damaging the core? | The sacrifice is performative, irreversible, or too expensive. |
| 35 | 連環計 | Chain constraints so system dependencies reinforce the desired outcome. | Which linked moves make each other stronger? | The chain creates fragility or hidden correlated risk. |
| 36 | 走為上計 | Exit preserves optionality; retreat can be the highest-quality move. | Is stopping, exiting, or going to cash the best strategic move? | Exit locks in avoidable loss while viable recovery remains. |
