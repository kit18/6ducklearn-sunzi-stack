# Strategy Focus Group Evaluation

Use this reference when applying `sunzi-focus-group-skill-review` to the real-world strategy cases in this stack.

## Source Anchors

- Harro von Senger, [`The 36 Stratagems for Business`](https://books.google.com/books/about/The_36_Stratagems_for_Business.html?id=WbPenQEACAAJ): Thirty-Six Stratagems applied to business and economic competition.
- Beijing Review, [`Harro von Senger and His 36 Stratagems`](https://www.bjreview.com/books/txt/2006-12/21/content_93832_2.htm): stratagem literacy for politics, economics, human relations, trap detection, and observation.
- Gerald and Steven Michaelson, [`Sun Tzu Strategies for Marketing`](https://books.google.com/books/about/Sun_Tzu_Strategies_for_Marketing_12_Esse.html?id=q_OR3p2VV4gC): Sun Tzu applied to customer and market strategy.
- Gerald and Steven Michaelson, [`Sun Tzu: The Art of War for Managers`](https://books.google.com/books/about/Sun_Tzu_The_Art_of_War_for_Managers.html?id=IujsDQAAQBAJ): Sun Tzu applied to management and leadership.
- Gerald and Steven Michaelson, [`Sun Tzu for Success`](https://www.barnesandnoble.com/w/sun-tzu-for-success-gerald-a-michaelson/1111956625): Sun Tzu applied to personal goals, adversity, and achievement.
- Hiroshi Moriya, [`The 36 Strategies of the Martial Arts`](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.ca/books/223511/the-36-strategies-of-the-martial-arts-by-hiroshi-moriya/9781590309926): Thirty-Six Stratagems applied to war, business, and life.
- [`管人36計`](https://www.books.com.tw/products/0010937872): talent management through Sunzi and Thirty-Six Stratagems framing.
- [`職場孫子兵法`](https://www.eslite.com/product/10072302132682753162006): workplace, management, negotiation, and business applications.

## Focus Group Method

Run a synthetic panel simulation, not real quotes. Use at least these panel roles:

- PM lead
- UED researcher
- growth leader
- operations/SOP leader
- enterprise buyer
- platform engineer
- trust/safety reviewer
- customer-support lead

For each strategy:

1. Name the source anchor and real-world domain.
2. Define the DoD before reading panel reactions.
3. Generate at least four stakeholder reactions.
4. Score the first version as `go`, `revise`, or `stop`.
5. Preserve dissent.
6. Run Iteration 1 to address the highest-risk gap.
7. Run Iteration 2 to address adoption, safety, or evidence gaps.
8. Mark final state as `works after revision` only when DoD is satisfied.

## Safety Boundary

- Do not claim real industry leaders reviewed the strategy.
- Do not present synthetic stakeholder reactions as real quotes.
- Do not approve deception, coercion, hidden terms, fake proof, private-data misuse, or trust-destroying tactics.
- End with evidence needed next when the simulation remains uncertain.
