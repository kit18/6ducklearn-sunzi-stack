# Domain Adapters

Use the same first-principles map in every domain. Change the evidence lanes and output checks.

Deep adapters with full review contracts, DoD calibration, and mandatory 未戰 (pre-launch) lanes:

- [Growth and ecommerce](./domain-adapters/growth.md)
- [Operations and SOP](./domain-adapters/operations.md)
- [Product value and PRD](./domain-adapters/product.md)

The sections below are the quick-reference lane summaries.

## Growth and ecommerce

Inputs:
- Customer segment, funnel data, channel mix, conversion, retention, margin, inventory, service reliability, offer/pricing, and competitor pressure.

Required output:
- Binding growth constraint.
- 2 to 4 selected lenses.
- Test plan with owner, cohort, metric, and stop rule.
- Clear separation between demand creation, demand capture, and operational fulfillment.

Useful lens patterns:
- `擒賊擒王` for account, segment, or journey concentration.
- `圍魏救趙` when acquisition pain is really activation, delivery, or trust pain.
- `順手牽羊` for low-friction gains inside existing traffic.
- `偷樑換柱` for pricing metric, default, checkout, or channel architecture changes.

## Operations and supply chain

Inputs:
- Capacity, cycle time, inventory, yield, energy, labor, data visibility, supplier/customer volatility, quality, and operating cadence.

Required output:
- Physical bottleneck and information bottleneck.
- Decision latency and feedback metric.
- Intervention that changes process behavior, not just dashboards.
- Sustainability or resilience tradeoff when relevant.

Useful lens patterns:
- `釜底抽薪` for root cost, energy, waste, or manual-work drivers.
- `以逸待勞` for predictive planning instead of reactive firefighting.
- `連環計` for linked planning, data, governance, and capability moves.
- `調虎離山` when the bottleneck can move from buffers to information flow.

## AI and digital transformation

Inputs:
- Use cases, workflow pain, data readiness, adoption, governance, talent, platform constraints, user trust, and measurable business impact.

Required output:
- Dual-track plan: quick ROI use cases plus capability foundations.
- Human fallback and governance rules for high-stakes workflows.
- Adoption telemetry, not only training attendance.
- Build/buy/partner assumption.

Useful lens patterns:
- `借屍還魂` for modernizing legacy assets through new data or AI capability.
- `聲東擊西` for visible use cases that fund quieter platform foundations.
- `李代桃僵` for automation that protects human judgment in sensitive cases.
- `連環計` for platform, training, governance, and use-case loops.

## Macro and public sector

Inputs:
- Actors, policy levers, capital flows, institutional constraints, public trust, labor markets, sector competitiveness, regulation, and time horizon.

Required output:
- Policy or economic transmission mechanism.
- Who must change behavior and why.
- Leading and lagging metrics.
- Governance vehicle when normal institutions are too slow.

Useful lens patterns:
- `借刀殺人` for using private capital or third-party incentives toward public goals.
- `樹上開花` for amplifying existing regional strengths into credible investment narratives.
- `暗渡陳倉` for creating a new vehicle outside slow legacy process.
- `釜底抽薪` for removing leakage or friction from public systems.

## Trading, money markets, stocks, and crypto

Inputs:
- Business quality, cash flow, balance sheet, token economics, float/liquidity, volume, order book, catalysts, filings, social signals, funding, unlocks, and event calendar.

Required output:
- Value view, timing view, signal-quality view, manipulation-risk view, and risk view.
- Verdict: avoid, watch, pullback entry candidate, event trade candidate, or value candidate.
- Invalidation and do-not-chase level.
- Clear statement that analysis is not financial advice.

Useful lens patterns:
- `瞞天過海` for hidden distribution under normal-looking promotion or volume.
- `暗渡陳倉` for real trade hidden behind visible liquidity.
- `無中生有` for false supply/demand signals.
- `走為上計` when cash, stop, or no-trade is the best decision.
