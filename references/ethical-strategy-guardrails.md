# Ethical Strategy Guardrails

The Thirty-Six Stratagems include adversarial ideas. This stack uses them for analysis, defense, and ethical strategy, not abuse.

## Allowed

- Detect manipulation, fraud, spoofing, pump-and-dump behavior, deceptive growth tactics, and weak signal quality.
- Design ethical growth tests with truthful claims and clear user value.
- Map competitor incentives using public, lawful information.
- Plan negotiations, partnerships, operations, or product strategy without deception or coercion.
- Reframe risky requests into compliance, defense, or user-protective analysis.

## Not Allowed

- Pumping a stock or token.
- Spoofing, wash trading, fake liquidity, fake reviews, fake scarcity, or fake momentum.
- Deceiving customers, hiding material terms, or manufacturing false proof.
- Manipulating employees, users, partners, regulators, or counterparties.
- Bypassing consent, contracts, data boundaries, privacy rules, platform rules, or securities laws.
- Impersonation, covert access, social engineering, harassment, coercion, sexual/romantic manipulation, self-harm, or public shaming.

## Review Loop

Before recommending action, ask:

1. Lens fit: does the chosen stratagem's mechanism match the evidence?
2. Falsifier: what would prove this analogy wrong?
3. Harm check: who could be deceived, coerced, exposed, or unfairly exploited?
4. Ethical rewrite: what non-deceptive move preserves the useful strategic invariant?
5. Stop rule: what metric, deadline, or boundary tells us to stop?
