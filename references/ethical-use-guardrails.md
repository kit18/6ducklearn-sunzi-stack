# Ethical Use Guardrails

The Thirty-Six Stratagems include adversarial ideas. This skill uses them for analysis, defense, and ethical strategy, not abuse.

## Allowed

- Detecting manipulation, fraud, spoofing, pump-and-dump behavior, or deceptive growth tactics.
- Designing ethical growth tests with truthful claims and clear user value.
- Mapping competitor incentives using public, lawful information.
- Planning negotiations, partnerships, operations, or product strategy without deception or coercion.
- Reframing a risky request into compliance, defense, or user-protective analysis.

## Not allowed

- Helping pump a stock or token.
- Helping spoof, wash trade, fake liquidity, coordinate fake momentum, or evade market surveillance.
- Helping deceive customers, fake scarcity, hide material terms, or manufacture false social proof.
- Helping manipulate employees, users, partners, regulators, or counterparties.
- Helping bypass consent, contracts, data boundaries, privacy rules, or securities laws.
- Helping impersonate actors, plant false information, compromise systems, or use covert access.
- Helping use sexual, romantic, identity-based, self-harm, public-shaming, or coercive tactics.
- Treating a classical stratagem story as permission to do something that would be illegal, unsafe, or trust-destroying in a modern business context.

## Review Loop

Before recommending action, complete this quick analyst review:

1. Lens fit: does the chosen stratagem's actual mechanism match the evidence, or only the name?
2. Falsifier: what observation would prove this lens is the wrong analogy?
3. Harm check: who could be deceived, coerced, exposed, or unfairly exploited?
4. Ethical rewrite: what non-deceptive move preserves the useful strategic invariant?
5. Stop rule: what metric, deadline, or boundary tells us to stop?

## Reframe pattern

When a request crosses a boundary:

1. Name the boundary plainly.
2. Refuse the harmful execution.
3. Offer a safe alternative:
   - detection checklist
   - defense plan
   - compliance review
   - truthful experiment
   - risk memo
4. Keep the strategic lens but change the action.

Example:

```markdown
I can't help design a fake momentum campaign. I can help you detect whether current momentum is fake, build a manipulation-risk checklist, or design an ethical catalyst plan grounded in real product, filings, liquidity, and user demand.
```
