# Sunzi Stack Context

This repo publishes public-safe Sunzi and Thirty-Six Stratagems skills for modern strategy work. The domain language below is intentionally product- and business-facing, not runtime-specific.

## Glossary

### Sunzi Strategy Stack

The complete public skill set for strategic diagnosis, decision review, stakeholder simulation, growth review, operations/SOP review, product-value review, and analyst review. It is not a single PRD-review skill.

### Stakeholder Stress-Reaction Simulation

Abbreviated as SSR. A synthetic rehearsal method that produces stakeholder-style artifacts to expose hidden loss, fear, blame, escalation paths, technical blind spots, and safer responses before a strategy lands.

SSR output is a **hypothesis, not proof**. It can guide customer interviews, rollout planning, support readiness, and executive review, but it must not be presented as real research, real customer quotes, or validated market demand.

### Stress Lens

A canonical Thirty-Six Stratagems lens used to reveal pressure, not to prescribe manipulation. A stress lens must name the hidden mechanism it exposes and the safe response it implies.

### Office-Hours Demand Gate

The product-value gate based on demand reality, status quo, desperate specificity, narrowest wedge, observation, and future-fit. It belongs inside product-value review, while SSR is the cross-domain pressure test for growth, operations, product, policy, pricing, migration, and executive strategy.

### Strategy-Analyst Review

The final quality gate that audits evidence, assumptions, falsifiers, ethics, second-order effects, metrics, stop rules, and kill criteria before recommending action.

## Resolved Design Decisions

- The root optimization target is **strategy simulation and stakeholder stress testing**, not PRD review.
- PRD/product-value review remains one adapter alongside growth, operations/SOP, daily/professional decision review, and general strategic diagnosis.
- SSR examples must label synthetic artifacts clearly and must include a safe-response path.
- Case-study tests should pressure-test multiple SSR situations, not only API migration.
- Public examples must avoid private company names, customer records, secrets, internal identifiers, and hidden implementation details.
